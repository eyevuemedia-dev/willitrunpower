Questions

Can the BLUETTI AC200L run ResMed AirSense 10 with HumidAir and ClimateLineAir heated tube?

Runs. Draw is 34 W, within the 2,400 W continuous output. Estimated runtime: about 2.1 days.

How was the runtime calculated?

2,048 Wh × 0.85 = 1,741 Wh usable, divided by 34 W average draw.

Where did the numbers come from?

Spec block: 2,048 Wh (51.2 V, 40 Ah), 2,400 W total, Power Lifting 3,600 W, surge 7,200 W, 62.4 lb, LiFePO4, 3,000+ cycles to 80%. ResMed's therapy-device power table (ResMed Deutschland, Art.-Nr. 1018528/11) lists AirSense 10 AutoSet with HumidAir and heated ClimateLineAir tube at 34 Wh during operation. Energy per hour equals average watts, so the average draw is 34 W. ResMed notes the values were measured in a lab and that a local meter can differ.
