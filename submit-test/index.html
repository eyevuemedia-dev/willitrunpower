Submit a real-world power test

Measured your CPAP, fridge or space heater on a power station? Send the number and the proof. Willrun's runtimes come from nameplate watt-hours, an 85% usable allowance and about 15 W of inverter idle draw. Owner measurements are how we find out where that model is optimistic or conservative.

What counts as proof

  • A photo of a plug-in meter (Kill A Watt, Emporia, P3) showing watts or kWh, with the appliance in frame if you can.
  • A screenshot of the station's app (EcoFlow, Jackery, Anker, BLUETTI) showing output watts, or the start and end battery percentage for a runtime test.
  • Or a link to a video, forum post or log you published that shows the same numbers.

What happens next

  1. Your entry lands in a private review queue. Nothing is published automatically.
  2. We check the photo or link against your numbers, the product's published specs and our own estimate. Results that don't add up get an email question (if you left one) or are set aside.
  3. Verified results appear on the matching page as a Verified owner test, credited to the name or handle you gave, or "a reader" if you left it blank. Photos are only published after review, with personal details cropped or blurred.

Tips for a clean measurement

  • Note whether you ran from the AC outlet or the 12 V port. AC adds the inverter's own draw (roughly 10–20 W on most units), which matters a lot for small loads like a CPAP.
  • For runtime, start at 100%, record when the station shuts off or hits a set percentage, and note anything else plugged in.
  • Compressor appliances cycle. Log at least an hour, or use a meter's kWh total rather than one instantaneous reading.
