Questions

Can the Anker SOLIX F2000 (PowerHouse 767) run ResMed AirCurve 10 VAuto BiPAP with HumidAir and heated tube?

Runs. Draw is 41 W, within the 2,400 W continuous output. Estimated runtime: about 42.5 hr.

How was the runtime calculated?

2,048 Wh × 0.85 = 1,741 Wh usable, divided by 41 W average draw.

Where did the numbers come from?

Spec block for model A1780: 2,048 Wh, LFP, 3,000 cycles, AC output 2,400 W max. A separate surge rating and the weight were not in that spec block, so neither is used. A marketing line about 3,600 W was not in the spec table and is omitted. ResMed's therapy-device power table (ResMed Deutschland, Art.-Nr. 1018528/11) lists AirCurve 10 VAuto with HumidAir and heated ClimateLineAir tube at 41 Wh during operation. Energy per hour equals average watts, so the average draw is 41 W. ResMed notes the values were measured in a lab and that a local meter can differ.
