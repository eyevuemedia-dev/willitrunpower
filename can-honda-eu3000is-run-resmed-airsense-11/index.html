Questions

Can the Honda EU3000iS run ResMed AirSense 11, typical power from the user guide?

Runs. Running draw 56 W fits within 2,800 running watts, and published startup 73 W fits within 3,000 starting watts.

What watts were compared?

Generator: 2,800 running W and 3,000 starting W on gasoline. Appliance: 56 W running, 73 W startup.

How long will it run?

Fuel runtime depends on total load. Published runtime: 6.9 hr at rated load, 19.6 hr at 1/4 load.

Where did the numbers come from?

Honda spec table: 120V 3000W max (25A), 2800W rated (23.3A); 20A duplex and 30A 125V locking receptacle; 3.4 gal; 6.9 hr rated / 19.6 hr 1/4 load; 57/50 dB(A); 130.7 lb dry. The 30A outlet is an L5-30R, not a TT-30R. Americas user guide, 65 W power supply section: typical power consumption 56.1 W (111.5 VA) and peak power consumption 73.2 W (137.6 VA). This is the published system figure, not the lower no-humidifier row on ResMed's separate therapy-power sheet.

Safety first

  • Run a generator outdoors only, at least 20 feet from the house, with the exhaust pointed away from doors, windows and vents. Never in a garage, basement, shed or crawlspace, even with the door open.
  • Carbon monoxide is odorless and can kill in minutes. Keep battery CO alarms working inside the home.
  • Never backfeed a house by plugging a generator into a wall outlet or dryer receptacle. It can energize utility lines and kill a lineworker or a neighbor.
  • Connecting to home wiring requires a transfer switch or a listed breaker interlock with a power inlet box, installed by a licensed electrician and permitted where your jurisdiction requires it.
