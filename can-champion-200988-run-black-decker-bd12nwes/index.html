Can the Champion 4500W Dual Fuel Inverter (200988) run BLACK+DECKER BD12NWES 12,000 Btu/h window air conditioner?

Runs. Running draw 800 W fits within 3,500 running watts. Startup watts were not published; 4,500 starting watts is at least 3× the running draw, the common planning allowance for a compressor or pump start.

What watts were compared?

Generator: 3,500 running W and 4,500 starting W on gasoline. Appliance: 800 W running, startup not published.

How long will it run?

Fuel runtime depends on total load. Published runtime: 14 hr at 1/4 load on gasoline, 21 hr on a 20-lb propane tank.

Where did the numbers come from?

Champion spec table: gasoline 4500 starting / 3500 running; propane 4500 / 3150; TT-30R, 5-20R duplex, 12V DC; 2.25 gal; 14 hr gas / 21 hr propane at 1/4 load; 61 dBA. ENERGY STAR lists 12,000 Btu/h, CEER 15, 115 V, variable-speed compressor, and 600 kWh/year. Rated input is 12,000 ÷ 15 = 800 W, which matches 600 kWh at 750 hours. Startup watts are not published.

Safety first

  • Run a generator outdoors only, at least 20 feet from the house, with the exhaust pointed away from doors, windows and vents. Never in a garage, basement, shed or crawlspace, even with the door open.
  • Carbon monoxide is odorless and can kill in minutes. Keep battery CO alarms working inside the home.
  • Never backfeed a house by plugging a generator into a wall outlet or dryer receptacle. It can energize utility lines and kill a lineworker or a neighbor.
  • Connecting to home wiring requires a transfer switch or a listed breaker interlock with a power inlet box, installed by a licensed electrician and permitted where your jurisdiction requires it.
