Affiliate disclosure

As an Amazon Associate we earn from qualifying purchases. The "Check price on Amazon" buttons are affiliate links marked rel="sponsored"; a separate button opens the manufacturer page.

Questions

Does Willrun earn a commission?

Yes, through Amazon. As an Amazon Associate we earn from qualifying purchases made through the Check price on Amazon buttons.

How are affiliate links marked?

Every Amazon button uses rel="sponsored nofollow noopener" and names Amazon. The disclosure on this page still applies.

Do affiliate links change the verdict?

No. The runs, surge-risk, and won't-run results are computed from published watts. A paid link cannot add a station to a ranking or hide a surge risk.

Who publishes Willrun?
