Can the Westinghouse iGen4500DFc run Danby DAR026A1BDD 2.6 cu ft compact refrigerator?

Runs. Running draw 90 W fits within 3,700 running watts. Startup watts were not published; 4,500 starting watts is at least 3× the running draw, the common planning allowance for a compressor or pump start.

What watts were compared?

Generator: 3,700 running W and 4,500 starting W on gasoline. Appliance: 90 W running, startup not published.

How long will it run?

Fuel runtime depends on total load. Published runtime: 18 hr at 25% load on gasoline, 22 hr at 25% on propane.

Where did the numbers come from?

Westinghouse spec sheet and manual: gas 4500 peak / 3700 running; propane 4050 / 3330; 5-20R duplex, TT-30R, 2 USB; 3.4 gal; 18 hr gas / 22 hr propane at 25%; 52 dBA; 105.8 lb dry; CO sensor. Danby lists 115 V, 90 W, and 253 kWh/year. Average draw is 28.9 W. Duty cycle 0.321 is the annual average implied by those two figures. Startup watts are not published.

Safety first

  • Run a generator outdoors only, at least 20 feet from the house, with the exhaust pointed away from doors, windows and vents. Never in a garage, basement, shed or crawlspace, even with the door open.
  • Carbon monoxide is odorless and can kill in minutes. Keep battery CO alarms working inside the home.
  • Never backfeed a house by plugging a generator into a wall outlet or dryer receptacle. It can energize utility lines and kill a lineworker or a neighbor.
  • Connecting to home wiring requires a transfer switch or a listed breaker interlock with a power inlet box, installed by a licensed electrician and permitted where your jurisdiction requires it.
