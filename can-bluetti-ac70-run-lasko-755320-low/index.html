Questions

Can the BLUETTI AC70 run Lasko 755320 ceramic heater, low?

Runs. The 900 W element is within the 1,000 W continuous output. A resistive load does not add a separate motor surge. Estimated runtime: about 44 min on a simple capacity estimate, or about 43 min counting about 15 W of inverter draw.

What watts were compared?

Station continuous output is 1,000 W. Motor surge used is 1,500 W. Appliance running draw is 900 W. Published appliance peak is 900 W.

How was the runtime calculated?

768 Wh × 0.85 = 653 Wh usable, divided by 900 W average draw. That is a simple estimate. On AC the station’s inverter also draws power while it is on: BLUETTI publishes about 15 W of self-consumption for the AC180, and an owner test measured about 17.5 W on the DELTA 3 Plus. Counting about 15 W, 653 Wh ÷ (900 W + 15 W) = about 43 min. The gap matters most for small loads.

Where did the numbers come from?

Spec table: 768 Wh, 1,000 W total, surge 1,500 W, about 22.5 lb, LiFePO4, 3,000+ cycles to 80%. Power Lifting to 2,000 W is described for heating devices. The FAQ says not to run an air conditioner or washing machine on lifting mode. A shelf price on the page was not recorded as a launch price. Same Lasko listing: energy output 900 W to 1,500 W. Low is the 900 W end of that range. 7.5 A × 120 V = 900 W.
