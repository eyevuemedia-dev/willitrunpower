Questions

Can the Anker SOLIX F2000 (PowerHouse 767) run Philips DreamStation, worst case at the 80 W power supply rating?

Runs. Draw is 80 W, within the 2,400 W continuous output. Estimated runtime: about 21.8 hr.

How was the runtime calculated?

2,048 Wh × 0.85 = 1,741 Wh usable, divided by 80 W average draw.

Where did the numbers come from?

Spec block for model A1780: 2,048 Wh, LFP, 3,000 cycles, AC output 2,400 W max. A separate surge rating and the weight were not in that spec block, so neither is used. A marketing line about 3,600 W was not in the spec table and is omitted. Philips does not publish a typical DreamStation draw. The DreamStation Heated Humidifier manual lists the 80 W power supply and DC power consumption of 12 VDC at 6.67 A (80 W). Willrun uses that ceiling as a worst case, so real nights will usually last longer. Heated tubing is powered through the humidifier.
