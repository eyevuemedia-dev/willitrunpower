Can the WEN 56380i run GE PWJV10 10,000 Btu/h window air conditioner?

Runs. Running draw 625 W fits within 3,400 running watts. Startup watts were not published; 3,800 starting watts is at least 3× the running draw, the common planning allowance for a compressor or pump start.

What watts were compared?

Generator: 3,400 running W and 3,800 starting W on gasoline. Appliance: 625 W running, startup not published.

How long will it run?

Fuel runtime depends on total load. Published runtime: 8.5 hr at 50% load.

Where did the numbers come from?

WEN product page and manual: 3400 running / 3800 surge; TT-30R and two 5-20R; 2.2 gal; 8.5 hr at 50%; 99.2 lb. ENERGY STAR lists model PWJV10W**# at 10,000 Btu/h, CEER 16, and 468.8 kWh/year, with a variable-speed compressor. Rated input is 10,000 ÷ 16 = 625 W. 625 × 750 ÷ 1,000 = 468.75, which rounds to the listed 468.8 kWh. Startup watts are not published.

Safety first

  • Run a generator outdoors only, at least 20 feet from the house, with the exhaust pointed away from doors, windows and vents. Never in a garage, basement, shed or crawlspace, even with the door open.
  • Carbon monoxide is odorless and can kill in minutes. Keep battery CO alarms working inside the home.
  • Never backfeed a house by plugging a generator into a wall outlet or dryer receptacle. It can energize utility lines and kill a lineworker or a neighbor.
  • Connecting to home wiring requires a transfer switch or a listed breaker interlock with a power inlet box, installed by a licensed electrician and permitted where your jurisdiction requires it.
