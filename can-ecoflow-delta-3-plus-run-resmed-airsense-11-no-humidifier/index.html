Questions

Can the EcoFlow DELTA 3 Plus run ResMed AirSense 11 AutoSet, no humidifier?

Runs. Draw is 7 W, within the 1,800 W continuous output. Estimated runtime: about 5.2 days.

How was the runtime calculated?

1,024 Wh × 0.85 = 870 Wh usable, divided by 7 W average draw.

Where did the numbers come from?

US spec table: 1,024 Wh, 6 outlets, 1,800 W (surge 3,600 W), X-Boost 2,200 W, about 12.5 kg (27.6 lb), LFP, 4,000 cycles to 80%+. ResMed's therapy-device power table lists AirSense 11 AutoSet operation at 7 Wh and standby at 2 Wh. The operation figure is energy per hour, so the average draw is 7 W. ResMed notes the values were measured in a lab and that a local meter can differ.
