Can the Anker SOLIX F2000 (PowerHouse 767) run ResMed AirCurve 11 VAuto BiPAP, no humidifier?

Runs

Runs. Draw is 8 W, within the 2,400 W continuous output. Estimated runtime on AC: about 3.2 days, counting about 15 W of inverter draw.

Medical safety notice: Will It Run? gives general power estimates only, not medical advice. Power draw varies by model, settings, pressure, humidifier and altitude. Never change prescribed settings to extend battery life. If your therapy is life-sustaining, keep the backup your supplier provides, register with your utility's medical-needs program, and have an evacuation plan. How AC runtime is figured: usable Wh (nameplate × 0.85) ÷ (CPAP watts + about 15 W). The 15 W is the inverter's own draw just to stay on: BLUETTI counts about 15 W of self-consumption in its AC180 runtime formula, and an owner test measured about 17.5 W idle on a DELTA 3 Plus. Larger stations can idle higher, so test your own setup before you rely on it. DC figures skip the inverter and are a best case. Method and DC converter limits: CPAP power station guide.

Questions

Can the Anker SOLIX F2000 (PowerHouse 767) run ResMed AirCurve 11 VAuto BiPAP, no humidifier?

Runs. Draw is 8 W, within the 2,400 W continuous output. Estimated runtime on AC: about 3.2 days, counting about 15 W of inverter draw.

How was the runtime calculated?

On AC: 2,048 Wh × 0.85 = 1,741 Wh usable, divided by (8 W + about 15 W inverter draw) = 3.2 days. On DC with the ResMed Air11 DC/DC converter in the car port there is no inverter, so the best case is 1,741 Wh divided by 8 W = 9.1 days.

Where did the numbers come from?

Spec block for model A1780: 2,048 Wh, LFP, 3,000 cycles, AC output 2,400 W max. A separate surge rating and the weight were not in that spec block, so neither is used. A marketing line about 3,600 W was not in the spec table and is omitted. ResMed's therapy-device power table (ResMed Deutschland, Art.-Nr. 1018528/11) lists AirCurve 11 VAuto at 8 Wh during operation. Energy per hour equals average watts, so the average draw is 8 W. ResMed notes the values were measured in a lab and that a local meter can differ.
