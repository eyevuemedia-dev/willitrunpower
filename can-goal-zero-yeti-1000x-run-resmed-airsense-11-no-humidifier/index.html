Can the Goal Zero Yeti 1000X run ResMed AirSense 11 AutoSet, no humidifier?

Runs. Draw is 7 W, within the 1,500 W continuous output. Estimated runtime: about 5.0 days.

How was the runtime calculated?

997 Wh × 0.85 = 847 Wh usable, divided by 7 W average draw.

Where did the numbers come from?

Battery block: 997 Wh, NMC, 500 cycles to 80% at 1C, 31.68 lb. The ports section says 1,500 W and 3,500 W surge; a diagram label on the same page says 1,500 W and 3,000 W surge. The surge is omitted because the page disagrees with itself. Continuous 1,500 W is consistent. ResMed's therapy-device power table lists AirSense 11 AutoSet operation at 7 Wh and standby at 2 Wh. The operation figure is energy per hour, so the average draw is 7 W. ResMed notes the values were measured in a lab and that a local meter can differ.
