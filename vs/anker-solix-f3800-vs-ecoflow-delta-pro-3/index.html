Questions

How do the Anker SOLIX F3800 and the EcoFlow DELTA Pro 3 compare?

EcoFlow DELTA Pro 3 stores more energy (4,096 Wh), and Anker SOLIX F3800 has the higher continuous output (6,000 W).

Which one runs longer?

Anker SOLIX F3800 has 3,264 Wh usable in Willrun's math (3,840 Wh × 0.85). EcoFlow DELTA Pro 3 has 3,482 Wh usable. Runtime still depends on the appliance's average draw.

Can cycle life be ranked between them?

The cycle quotes do not share a published endpoint, or one of them is missing. Willrun does not rank 70% retention against 80% retention.

Where do the numbers come from?

Tech specs: 3,840 Wh, LFP with 3,000+ cycles, 6,000 W continuous at 120 V and 240 V, 9,000 W AC surge, net weight 132.3 lb including the wheel dimension noted on the page. US spec block: 4,096 Wh, AC continuous 4,000 W and 6,000 W with X-Boost, AC surge 8,000 W, 51.5 kg (113.54 lb), LFP, 4,000 cycles to 80%.
