Questions

How do the EcoFlow RIVER 2 Pro and the BLUETTI AC70 compare?

BLUETTI AC70 has the higher continuous output (1,000 W).

Which one runs longer?

EcoFlow RIVER 2 Pro has 653 Wh usable in Willrun's math (768 Wh × 0.85). BLUETTI AC70 has 653 Wh usable. Runtime still depends on the appliance's average draw.

Can cycle life be ranked between them?

The cycle quotes do not share a published endpoint, or one of them is missing. Willrun does not rank 70% retention against 80% retention.

Where do the numbers come from?

Product spec: 768 Wh, pure sine wave, 800 W total (surge 1,600 W). Comparison table lists the same 1,600 W as X-Boost, 4 AC outlets, and 17.2 lb. Treated as resistive boost, not motor-start capacity. Spec table: 768 Wh, 1,000 W total, surge 1,500 W, about 22.5 lb, LiFePO4, 3,000+ cycles to 80%. Power Lifting to 2,000 W is described for heating devices. The FAQ says not to run an air conditioner or washing machine on lifting mode. A shelf price on the page was not recorded as a launch price.
