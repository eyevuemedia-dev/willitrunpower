Questions

How do the EcoFlow DELTA 2 Max and the Anker SOLIX F2000 (PowerHouse 767) compare?

EcoFlow DELTA 2 Max and Anker SOLIX F2000 (PowerHouse 767) publish the same capacity and continuous output, so the useful differences are chemistry, surge, weight, and ports.

Which one runs longer?

EcoFlow DELTA 2 Max has 1,741 Wh usable in Willrun's math (2,048 Wh × 0.85). Anker SOLIX F2000 (PowerHouse 767) has 1,741 Wh usable. Runtime still depends on the appliance's average draw.

Can cycle life be ranked between them?

The cycle quotes do not share a published endpoint, or one of them is missing. Willrun does not rank 70% retention against 80% retention.

Where do the numbers come from?

US spec table: 2,048 Wh, 6 outlets, 2,400 W total (surge 4,800 W), X-Boost 3,400 W, 50 lb, LFP, 3,000 cycles to 80%+. Surge and X-Boost are separate numbers, so the 4,800 W surge is used for motor checks. Spec block for model A1780: 2,048 Wh, LFP, 3,000 cycles, AC output 2,400 W max. A separate surge rating and the weight were not in that spec block, so neither is used. A marketing line about 3,600 W was not in the spec table and is omitted.
