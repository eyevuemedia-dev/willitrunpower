Runs, surge risk. Running draw 800 W is within 1,800 W continuous, but startup watts were not published. Willrun will not clear a compressor, pump, or tool start without that number. Estimated runtime: about 1.1 hr.

What watts were compared?

Station continuous output is 1,800 W. Motor surge used is 2,400 W. Appliance running draw is 800 W.

How was the runtime calculated?

1,056 Wh × 0.85 = 898 Wh usable, divided by 800 W average draw.

Where did the numbers come from?

Tech specs: 1,056 Wh, LiFePO4, 3,000 cycles to 80%, 1,800 W pure sine wave, 2,400 W AC surge, 6 AC outlets, 28.44 lb. The FAQ says SurgePad is for heat-generating devices when output exceeds the rated output; the 2,400 W figure is the published AC surge. ENERGY STAR lists 12,000 Btu/h, CEER 15, 115 V, variable-speed compressor, and 600 kWh/year. Rated input is 12,000 ÷ 15 = 800 W, which matches 600 kWh at 750 hours. Startup watts are not published.
