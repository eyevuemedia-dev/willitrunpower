Questions

Can the Champion 7500W Dual Fuel (100891) run BLACK+DECKER BDM05C 5,000 Btu/h window air conditioner?

Runs. Running draw 347 W fits within 7,500 running watts. Startup watts were not published; 9,375 starting watts is at least 3× the running draw, the common planning allowance for a compressor or pump start.

What watts were compared?

Generator: 7,500 running W and 9,375 starting W on gasoline. Appliance: 347 W running, startup not published.

How long will it run?

Fuel runtime depends on total load. Published runtime: 10 hr at 1/2 load on gasoline, 5.5 hr at 1/2 load on a 20-lb propane tank.

Where did the numbers come from?

Champion operator's manual: gasoline 9375 starting / 7500 running; propane 8400 / 6750; L14-30R and L5-30R; 7.7 gal; 10 hr gas / 5.5 hr propane at 50% load. ENERGY STAR lists 5,000 Btu/h, CEER 14.4, 115 V, variable-speed compressor, and 260.4 kWh/year. Rated input is 5,000 ÷ 14.4 = 347.2 W. That matches the listed annual energy at the DOE room-AC assumption of 750 hours (347.2 × 750 ÷ 1,000 = 260.4). The 347.2 W figure is the draw while cooling at the rated condition, not a whole-year average. Startup watts are not published.

Safety first

  • Run a generator outdoors only, at least 20 feet from the house, with the exhaust pointed away from doors, windows and vents. Never in a garage, basement, shed or crawlspace, even with the door open.
  • Carbon monoxide is odorless and can kill in minutes. Keep battery CO alarms working inside the home.
  • Never backfeed a house by plugging a generator into a wall outlet or dryer receptacle. It can energize utility lines and kill a lineworker or a neighbor.
  • Connecting to home wiring requires a transfer switch or a listed breaker interlock with a power inlet box, installed by a licensed electrician and permitted where your jurisdiction requires it.
