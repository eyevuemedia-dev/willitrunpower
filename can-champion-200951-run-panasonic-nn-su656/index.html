Can the Champion 2500W Ultralight Inverter (200951) run Panasonic NN-SU656WAWH microwave, electrical input?

Runs. Running draw 1,500 W fits within 1,850 running watts and this is not a motor-start load.

What watts were compared?

Generator: 1,850 running W and 2,500 starting W on gasoline. Appliance: 1,500 W running, startup not published.

How long will it run?

Fuel runtime depends on total load. Published runtime: 11.5 hr at 1/4 load.

Where did the numbers come from?

Champion operator's manual and sell sheet: 2500 starting / 1850 running, 79cc, 1.1 gal, 11.5 hr at 25% load, 39 lb. Outlet detail beyond 120V household outlets not verified. Panasonic lists power supply 120 V / 60 Hz, microwave output 1,100 W, and microwave power consumption 1,500 W. The station has to supply the 1,500 W input, not the 1,100 W cooking output. A separate startup surge is not published. X-Boost and Power Lifting are not applied, because those modes are described for simple heating loads and a microwave is an electronic supply.

Safety first

  • Run a generator outdoors only, at least 20 feet from the house, with the exhaust pointed away from doors, windows and vents. Never in a garage, basement, shed or crawlspace, even with the door open.
  • Carbon monoxide is odorless and can kill in minutes. Keep battery CO alarms working inside the home.
  • Never backfeed a house by plugging a generator into a wall outlet or dryer receptacle. It can energize utility lines and kill a lineworker or a neighbor.
  • Connecting to home wiring requires a transfer switch or a listed breaker interlock with a power inlet box, installed by a licensed electrician and permitted where your jurisdiction requires it.
