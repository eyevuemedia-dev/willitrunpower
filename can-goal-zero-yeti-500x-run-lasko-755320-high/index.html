Questions

Can the Goal Zero Yeti 500X run Lasko 755320 ceramic heater, high?

Won't run. 1,500 W is above the 300 W continuous output. Estimated runtime: no runtime, because it will not stay on.

What watts were compared?

Station continuous output is 300 W. Motor surge used is 1,200 W. Appliance running draw is 1,500 W. Published appliance peak is 1,500 W.

How was the runtime calculated?

497 Wh × 0.85 = 422 Wh usable, divided by 1,500 W average draw.

Where did the numbers come from?

Spec list: 497 Wh, Li-ion NMC, 500 cycles to 80% at 0.2C, 12.9 lb, 2× 120 V AC at 300 W and 1,200 W surge. A shelf price on the page was not recorded as a launch price. Lasko lists 120 V, current 7.5 A to 12.5 A, and energy output 900 W to 1,500 W. High is 1,500 W. A resistive element does not have a separate motor surge, so surge is set equal to the high setting. The thermostat cycles in real rooms; this entry assumes the high element is on.
