Questions

Can the Anker SOLIX F3800 run Zoeller Model 53 3/10 HP sump pump, 115 V?

Runs, surge risk. Running draw 1,116 W is within 6,000 W continuous, but startup watts were not published. Willrun will not clear a compressor, pump, or tool start without that number. Estimated runtime: about 2.9 hr.

What watts were compared?

Station continuous output is 6,000 W. Motor surge used is 9,000 W. Appliance running draw is 1,116 W.

How was the runtime calculated?

3,840 Wh × 0.85 = 3,264 Wh usable, divided by 1,116 W average draw.

Where did the numbers come from?

Tech specs: 3,840 Wh, LFP with 3,000+ cycles, 6,000 W continuous at 120 V and 240 V, 9,000 W AC surge, net weight 132.3 lb including the wheel dimension noted on the page. Zoeller lists the Model 53 at 3/10 HP, 115 V or 230 V, and 4.8–9.7 A. The 115 V automatic model in Zoeller's 50-series technical table is 9.7 A. Running watts used here are 9.7 × 115 = 1,115.5 W. Startup amps are not published. How often the float runs is site-specific, so no duty cycle is stored.
