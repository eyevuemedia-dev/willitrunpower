Guide

CPAP during a power outage

Turn the humidifier and heated tube off during an outage if your doctor agrees. On ResMed's figures that cuts nightly use from about 440 Wh to about 56 Wh.

Medical safety notice: Will It Run? gives general power estimates only, not medical advice. Power draw varies by model, settings, pressure, humidifier and altitude. Before relying on any battery, power station, inverter or generator for a medical device, confirm approved power sources with the device manufacturer and your doctor or equipment supplier, and test your setup before an emergency. Never change prescribed settings to extend battery life. If your therapy is life-sustaining, keep the backup your supplier provides, register with your utility's medical-needs program, and have an evacuation plan. Run generators outdoors only, at least 20 ft from windows and doors, because of carbon monoxide risk. In an emergency, call 911.

Plan it before the storm

  1. Find your exact machine and setting below and note the watt-hours per night.
  2. If it will run on AC, add about 15 W for the station's inverter, multiply by 8 hours and the nights you want to cover, then divide by 0.85 to get the station size. On DC, skip the 15 W.
  3. Charge the station and test a full night on it before you need it.
  4. Ask your supplier whether your machine has a DC cord, which can stretch runtime.
  5. A generator can recharge the station, but only run it outdoors, far from windows and doors.

Questions

How do I power a CPAP during a power outage?

Use a portable power station sized for your nightly watt-hours, or your machine's DC cord with a suitable battery. Confirm approved sources with your supplier.

What happens to a CPAP when the power goes out?

It stops delivering pressure unless it is on a battery or power station. Keep your mask on only if the machine is running.
