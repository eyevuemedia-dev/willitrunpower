Questions

Can the EcoFlow RIVER 2 Pro run ResMed AirCurve 11 VAuto BiPAP with HumidAir and heated tube?

Runs. Draw is 66 W, within the 800 W continuous output. Estimated runtime: about 9.9 hr.

How was the runtime calculated?

768 Wh × 0.85 = 653 Wh usable, divided by 66 W average draw.

Where did the numbers come from?

Product spec: 768 Wh, pure sine wave, 800 W total (surge 1,600 W). Comparison table lists the same 1,600 W as X-Boost, 4 AC outlets, and 17.2 lb. Treated as resistive boost, not motor-start capacity. ResMed's therapy-device power table (ResMed Deutschland, Art.-Nr. 1018528/11) lists AirCurve 11 VAuto with HumidAir and heated ClimateLineAir tube at 66 Wh during operation. Energy per hour equals average watts, so the average draw is 66 W. ResMed notes the values were measured in a lab and that a local meter can differ.
