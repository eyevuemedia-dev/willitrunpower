How was the runtime calculated?

1,264.64 Wh × 0.85 = 1,075 Wh usable, divided by 22 W average draw.

Where did the numbers come from?

Spec block: 30.4 Ah / 41.6 V DC (1,264.64 Wh), LiFePO4, 4,000 cycles to 70%+, 32 lb, AC total 2,000 W rated and 4,000 W surge peak. ResMed's therapy-device power table (ResMed Deutschland, Art.-Nr. 1018528/11) lists AirSense 10 AutoSet with HumidAir at 22 Wh during operation. Energy per hour equals average watts, so the average draw is 22 W. ResMed notes the values were measured in a lab and that a local meter can differ.
