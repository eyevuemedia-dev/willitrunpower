Questions

Can the Generac GP3500iO run Danby DCR032C1WDB 3.2 cu ft compact refrigerator?

Runs. Running draw 66 W fits within 3,000 running watts. Startup watts were not published; 3,500 starting watts is at least 3× the running draw, the common planning allowance for a compressor or pump start.

What watts were compared?

Generator: 3,000 running W and 3,500 starting W on gasoline. Appliance: 66 W running, startup not published.

How long will it run?

Fuel runtime depends on total load. Published runtime: 11 hr at 25% load, 8 hr at 50% load.

Where did the numbers come from?

Generac spec sheet (7128): 3000 W running, 3500 W starting; L5-30R with 30A breaker; 2.37 gal; 11 hr at 25%, 8 hr at 50%. Danby lists 115 V, 66.4 W, and 218 kWh/year. Average draw is 218 × 1,000 ÷ 8,760 = 24.9 W. Duty cycle 0.375 is that average divided by 66.4 W, so it is an annual-average compressor fraction, not a hot-day measurement. Startup watts are not published.

Safety first

  • Run a generator outdoors only, at least 20 feet from the house, with the exhaust pointed away from doors, windows and vents. Never in a garage, basement, shed or crawlspace, even with the door open.
  • Carbon monoxide is odorless and can kill in minutes. Keep battery CO alarms working inside the home.
  • Never backfeed a house by plugging a generator into a wall outlet or dryer receptacle. It can energize utility lines and kill a lineworker or a neighbor.
  • Connecting to home wiring requires a transfer switch or a listed breaker interlock with a power inlet box, installed by a licensed electrician and permitted where your jurisdiction requires it.
