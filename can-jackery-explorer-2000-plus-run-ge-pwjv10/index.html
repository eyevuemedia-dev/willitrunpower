Questions

Can the Jackery Explorer 2000 Plus run GE PWJV10 10,000 Btu/h window air conditioner?

Runs, surge risk. Running draw 625 W is within 3,000 W continuous, but startup watts were not published. Willrun will not clear a compressor, pump, or tool start without that number. Estimated runtime: about 2.8 hr on a simple capacity estimate, or about 2.7 hr counting about 15 W of inverter draw.

What watts were compared?

Station continuous output is 3,000 W. Motor surge used is 6,000 W. Appliance running draw is 625 W.

How was the runtime calculated?

2,042.8 Wh × 0.85 = 1,736 Wh usable, divided by 625 W average draw. That is a simple estimate. On AC the station’s inverter also draws power while it is on: BLUETTI publishes about 15 W of self-consumption for the AC180, and an owner test measured about 17.5 W on the DELTA 3 Plus. Counting about 15 W, 1,736 Wh ÷ (625 W + 15 W) = about 2.7 hr. The gap matters most for small loads.

Where did the numbers come from?

Spec block: 45.6 Ah / 44.8 V DC (2,042.8 Wh), LiFePO4, 4,000 cycles to 70%+, 61.5 lb, AC total 3,000 W max and 6,000 W surge peak. ENERGY STAR lists model PWJV10W**# at 10,000 Btu/h, CEER 16, and 468.8 kWh/year, with a variable-speed compressor. Rated input is 10,000 ÷ 16 = 625 W. 625 × 750 ÷ 1,000 = 468.75, which rounds to the listed 468.8 kWh. Startup watts are not published.
