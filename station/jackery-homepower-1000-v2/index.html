Will it start a fridge?

Willrun only trusts a full-size fridge when the station publishes a motor-start surge of at least 1,200 W. Boost modes for heaters do not count. Jackery lists a 3,000 W maximum instantaneous / surge peak on the HomePower 1000 v2 — the same class of number as the Explorer 1000 v2 — so it clears the rule. UPS / switchover and home use

Jackery sells the HomePower line as a home UPS: FAQ UPS timing is 10 ms, with pass-through charging and a bypass that can pass up to 12 A from the wall while the battery stays topped up (manual). That is in the same band as EcoFlow's <10 ms units and faster than the Explorer 1000 v2's <20 ms. Priority Routing can shed non-priority outlets when the battery hits a threshold you set — useful in a long outage if the fridge and router must outlast a TV.

Turn Energy Saving Mode off (app / settings) when a low continuous load must not time out. Test before you need it: with the device running, unplug the station from the wall and make sure nothing reboots.

Ports, weight and cycles

Three AC outlets share the 1,500 W inverter. USB-C tops out at 100 W and 30 W, plus one 18 W USB-A and a 12 V / 10 A car port — enough to charge a laptop and phones without waking the inverter. At 23.4 lb it is a hair lighter than the Explorer 1000 v2 (23.8 lb) and about five pounds under the C1000 (28.44 lb), which matters if it lives in a closet and comes out for camping.

Cycle life is the clearest differentiator inside Jackery's 1 kWh pair: 6,000 cycles to ≥70% vs 4,000 on the Explorer 1000 v2 (both to 70%, so they flatter vs brands that rate to 80%). Jackery's marketing frames that as ~16 years of daily cycling; treat it as a cell rating, not a field measurement. Cost per rated kWh of life still looks strong even at today's $559 black price (~$0.09 / kWh of rated life using 1.024 kWh × 6,000), vs roughly $0.13 on the Explorer at the same shelf price with 4,000 cycles.

Questions

How long will the Jackery HomePower 1000 v2 run a refrigerator?

About 15 hours on a median ENERGY STAR top-freezer (~41 W average) and about 11 hours on a median bottom-freezer, using usable energy of 870 Wh (1,024 × 0.85) and about 15 W of inverter draw. The published 3,000 W surge peak clears Willrun's ≥1,200 W motor-start rule for a full-size fridge.

How long will the Jackery HomePower 1000 v2 run a CPAP?

On AC, a ResMed AirSense 11 with humidifier and heated tube at about 55 W runs roughly 12.4 hours (about 1.6 nights). Without heat, several times that. Jackery says its power stations are not recommended for medical devices; confirm any backup plan with your doctor. Affiliate commissions do not shape compatibility claims.

Is the HomePower 1000 v2 a UPS?

Jackery rates UPS switchover at 10 ms and sells a bypass mode that passes up to 12 A from the wall while the battery stays topped up. That is fast enough for most routers, modems, NAS boxes and many desktops. Turn Energy Saving Mode off if a low load must stay powered.

HomePower 1000 v2 vs Explorer 1000 v2 vs Anker SOLIX C1000?

HomePower and Explorer share 1,500 W / 3,000 W surge and 400 W solar; HomePower is 1,024 Wh at 23.4 lb with 6,000 cycles to 70% and a 10 ms UPS, while Explorer is 1,070 Wh at 23.8 lb with 4,000 cycles and under 20 ms. The Anker C1000 is 1,056 Wh, 1,800 W continuous, 2,400 W surge, 600 W solar, expandable, and heavier at 28.44 lb.

What does the 0.85 factor do?

Willrun multiplies nameplate watt-hours by 0.85 before dividing by average draw. That allowance is for inverter loss. It is not a measured efficiency for this model.
