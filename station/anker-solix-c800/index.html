Questions

How long will the Anker SOLIX C800 run a CPAP?

Runs. At the AirSense 11 user-guide typical of 56.1 W, usable energy is 653 Wh and runtime is about 11.6 hr.

What does the 0.85 factor do?

Willrun multiplies nameplate watt-hours by 0.85 before dividing by average draw. That allowance is for inverter loss. It is not a measured efficiency for this model.

Is the surge rating safe to use for a fridge or a pump?

The extra headroom on this model is SurgePad. Willrun does not treat it as locked-rotor starting capacity.

Where do the station numbers come from?

User guide A1753: 768 Wh LiFePO4 cells, AC output 1,200 W max, max device power with SurgePad 1,600 W, net weight 23.1 lb. Cycle life was not in that table. SurgePad is not used as motor-start capacity.
