Questions

How long will the EcoFlow DELTA 2 Max run a CPAP?

Runs. At the AirSense 11 user-guide typical of 56.1 W, usable energy is 1,741 Wh and runtime is about 31.0 hr.

What does the 0.85 factor do?

Willrun multiplies nameplate watt-hours by 0.85 before dividing by average draw. That allowance is for inverter loss. It is not a measured efficiency for this model.

Is the surge rating safe to use for a fridge or a pump?

This model publishes a surge of 4,800 W separately from any heater-only boost, so Willrun uses it when the appliance also publishes startup watts. If the appliance does not, the verdict stays a surge risk.
