Questions

How long will the BLUETTI AC70 run a CPAP?

Runs. At the AirSense 11 user-guide typical of 56.1 W, usable energy is 653 Wh. On AC, adding about 15 W of inverter draw, runtime is about 9.2 hr. With the ResMed Air11 DC/DC converter in the car port (no inverter), the best case is about 11.6 hr.

What does the 0.85 factor do?

Willrun multiplies nameplate watt-hours by 0.85 before dividing by average draw. That allowance is for inverter loss. It is not a measured efficiency for this model.

Is the surge rating safe to use for a fridge or a pump?

This model publishes a surge of 1,500 W separately from any heater-only boost, so Willrun uses it when the appliance also publishes startup watts. If the appliance does not, the verdict stays a surge risk.
