Questions

Can the Generac GP8000E COsense run Amana AMND501BW dehumidifier, about 50 pints/day?

Runs. Running draw 485 W fits within 8,000 running watts. Startup watts were not published; 10,000 starting watts is at least 3× the running draw, the common planning allowance for a compressor or pump start.

What watts were compared?

Generator: 8,000 running W and 10,000 starting W on gasoline. Appliance: 485 W running, startup not published.

How long will it run?

Fuel runtime depends on total load. Published runtime: 15 hr at 25% load, 10 hr at 50% load.

Where did the numbers come from?

Generac spec sheet: 8000 W running, 10000 W starting; L14-30R with 2-pole 30A breaker; 15 hr at 25%, 10 hr at 50%; COsense shutdown. ENERGY STAR lists 49.43 pints/day, integrated energy factor 2.01 L/kWh, and 520 kWh/year. Operating draw at the rated test condition is (49.43 × 0.473176 L/pint ÷ 24 h) ÷ 2.01 L/kWh × 1,000 = 485 W. That is the draw while it is removing water at the rated rate, not a whole-year average. Startup watts are not published.

Safety first

  • Run a generator outdoors only, at least 20 feet from the house, with the exhaust pointed away from doors, windows and vents. Never in a garage, basement, shed or crawlspace, even with the door open.
  • Carbon monoxide is odorless and can kill in minutes. Keep battery CO alarms working inside the home.
  • Never backfeed a house by plugging a generator into a wall outlet or dryer receptacle. It can energize utility lines and kill a lineworker or a neighbor.
  • Connecting to home wiring requires a transfer switch or a listed breaker interlock with a power inlet box, installed by a licensed electrician and permitted where your jurisdiction requires it.
