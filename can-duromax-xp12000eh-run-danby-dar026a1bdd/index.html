Questions

Can the DuroMax XP12000EH run Danby DAR026A1BDD 2.6 cu ft compact refrigerator?

Runs. Running draw 90 W fits within 9,500 running watts. Startup watts were not published; 12,000 starting watts is at least 3× the running draw, the common planning allowance for a compressor or pump start.

What watts were compared?

Generator: 9,500 running W and 12,000 starting W on gasoline. Appliance: 90 W running, startup not published.

How long will it run?

Fuel runtime depends on total load. Published runtime: 19 hr at 25% / 8 hr at 50% on gasoline; 14 hr / 7 hr on a 40-lb propane tank.

Where did the numbers come from?

DuroMax product page and spec sheet: gasoline 12000 / 9500; propane 11400 / 9025; 120/240V 30A twist-lock and 50A; 19/8 hr gas and 14/7 hr propane (40-lb) at 25%/50%. Danby lists 115 V, 90 W, and 253 kWh/year. Average draw is 28.9 W. Duty cycle 0.321 is the annual average implied by those two figures. Startup watts are not published.

Safety first

  • Run a generator outdoors only, at least 20 feet from the house, with the exhaust pointed away from doors, windows and vents. Never in a garage, basement, shed or crawlspace, even with the door open.
  • Carbon monoxide is odorless and can kill in minutes. Keep battery CO alarms working inside the home.
  • Never backfeed a house by plugging a generator into a wall outlet or dryer receptacle. It can energize utility lines and kill a lineworker or a neighbor.
  • Connecting to home wiring requires a transfer switch or a listed breaker interlock with a power inlet box, installed by a licensed electrician and permitted where your jurisdiction requires it.
