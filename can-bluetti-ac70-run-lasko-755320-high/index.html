Questions

Can the BLUETTI AC70 run Lasko 755320 ceramic heater, high?

Runs. The 1,500 W element fits Power Lifting at 2,000 W, which is above the 1,000 W continuous rating. Voltage can sag in that mode, so a heater may put out less heat than the nameplate. Estimated runtime: about 26 min.

What watts were compared?

Station continuous output is 1,000 W. Motor surge used is 1,500 W. Appliance running draw is 1,500 W. Published appliance peak is 1,500 W.

How was the runtime calculated?

768 Wh × 0.85 = 653 Wh usable, divided by 1,500 W average draw.

Where did the numbers come from?

Spec table: 768 Wh, 1,000 W total, surge 1,500 W, about 22.5 lb, LiFePO4, 3,000+ cycles to 80%. Power Lifting to 2,000 W is described for heating devices. The FAQ says not to run an air conditioner or washing machine on lifting mode. A shelf price on the page was not recorded as a launch price. Lasko lists 120 V, current 7.5 A to 12.5 A, and energy output 900 W to 1,500 W. High is 1,500 W. A resistive element does not have a separate motor surge, so surge is set equal to the high setting. The thermostat cycles in real rooms; this entry assumes the high element is on.
