Best power station under $500: what $500 actually buys in 2026

$500 now buys about a kilowatt-hour of LiFePO4, which is enough to keep a router, phone, laptop and lights going for a day or a top-freezer fridge going overnight. If you can stretch to the ceiling, the Jackery Explorer 1000 v2 ($499, 1,070 Wh, 23.8 lb) and Anker SOLIX C1000 ($469.99 on sale, 1,056 Wh, 600 W solar, expandable) are the two in stock that publish a real motor-start surge, so they are the ones we'd trust with a fridge. If you only need to ride through outages at a desk or bedside, the EcoFlow RIVER 3 Plus ($299, 286 Wh, <10 ms switchover) is the better buy than a bigger box you'll rarely fill. The cheapest-per-Wh deals on paper, the BLUETTI AC180 and AC70, were out of stock at BLUETTI on October 24.

What $500 buys: three tiers

That second number is rough, since it ignores capacity fade and assumes full cycles, but it is why we steer away from old NMC stock.

Decision 1: will it start your fridge?

This is where cheap stations get oversold. A fridge compressor pulls several times its running watts for a fraction of a second at start, and a station that can't supply it trips off. Our rule from the fridge guide: a full-size fridge needs a published motor-start surge of at least 1,200 W. Boost modes don't count. EcoFlow X-Boost, BLUETTI Power Lifting and Anker SurgePad lower the output voltage so heaters and kettles keep working; BLUETTI's own AC180 FAQ says Power Lifting is not for air conditioners or washers.

What $500 won't do

  • A sump pump for long. A Zoeller Model 53 draws 1,115.5 W running. Only the C1000 and Jackery 1000 v2 have the continuous and motor surge for it, and 1 kWh covers about 48 minutes of pump run time. See sump pump backup.
  • 240 V loads. Well pumps, dryers and central AC need 240 V; none of these do it. See what size generator for a house.
  • A window AC overnight. An 8,000 Btu/h unit at about 533 W empties 1 kWh in well under two hours. See the RV guide.
  • Whole-home backup. For a multi-day outage across circuits, compare a generator vs a power station or look at the 2 kWh-plus picks in home backup.

Buying tips

  • Check stock before you fall for a spec sheet. On October 24 the two cheapest-per-Wh units (AC180, AC70) were out of stock at the maker. Retail and Amazon stock moves independently.
  • Compare the list price. The RIVER 2 Pro ($389 vs $529 list) and C1000 ($469.99 vs $599.99) are sale prices; sale cycles around holidays are common, so don't panic-buy at list.
  • Kits aren't always a deal. Price the bundled panel separately; folding panels from the same brand are often cheaper on their own.
  • Open-box and refurbished can be fine, but warranties are often shorter. Check before you buy.

Questions

What is the best power station under $500?

For most people, the Jackery Explorer 1000 v2 or Anker SOLIX C1000. Both are about 1 kWh of LiFePO4, both were in stock under $500 at the maker on October 24, 2026, and both publish a motor-start surge (3,000 W and 2,400 W) big enough to start a full-size fridge. Pick the Jackery if weight matters (23.8 lb vs 28.44 lb) and the Anker if you want more solar input (600 W vs 400 W) or the option to add a second battery.

Can a power station under $500 run a refrigerator?

Some can. A typical top-freezer fridge averages about 41 W (ENERGY STAR median 363 kWh/yr), so 1 kWh runs it roughly 16 hours on a Jackery 1000 v2 or Anker C1000. The catch is the compressor start: we only count stations whose published surge is a motor rating of at least 1,200 W. Boost modes like EcoFlow X-Boost and BLUETTI Power Lifting are for heaters and kettles, not compressors.

Is a $300 power station worth it?

Yes, if the job is small. The EcoFlow RIVER 3 Plus costs $299 for 286 Wh and switches to battery in under 10 ms, which is what a router, modem, NAS or desktop needs. It runs a 8.7 W router about 10 hours on AC, longer from its DC or USB ports. It will not run a fridge or a coffee maker for long; buy it as a UPS, not as home backup.

Will a power station under $500 run a CPAP all night?

Most will. A ResMed AirSense 11 with its humidifier and heated tube can pull about 55 W, which works out to about 1.2 nights on a 768 Wh RIVER 2 Pro and 1.6 nights on a 1,070 Wh Jackery on AC. Without heat, it's several times that. The 286 Wh RIVER 3 Plus won't get through a night with a heated tube on AC. Read the medical note on this page, check with your doctor, and see our CPAP guide for model-by-model nights and DC options.

Should I buy an older NMC power station on sale?

Usually no. The Goal Zero Yeti 500X lists $499.95 for 497 Wh of NMC rated for 500 cycles to 80%, which is about $1.01 per Wh and $2.01 per kWh of rated life. The ~770 Wh and 1 kWh LiFePO4 units here are $0.43 to $0.51 per Wh (the small RIVER 3 Plus is $1.05) and $0.12 to $0.35 per kWh of rated life. NMC is lighter, but you pay for it in lifespan.

Why are BLUETTI's cheapest models sold out?

We can't tell you why, only what BLUETTI's store showed on October 24, 2026: the AC180 out of stock with a late-November restock, and the AC70 out of stock with no current restock date. Amazon and other retailers may still have stock or open-box units; check the seller and that the warranty applies.
