What size generator for a 30 amp inlet

A 30 A, 120/240 V inlet tops out at 7,200 W, so a generator rated about 7,000–7,500 running watts with an L14-30R outlet uses the inlet fully. Typical essential circuits need about 4,440 W running and 6,790 W at the worst start.

Safety first

  • Run a generator outdoors only, at least 20 feet from the house, with the exhaust pointed away from doors, windows and vents. Never in a garage, basement, shed or crawlspace, even with the door open.
  • Carbon monoxide is odorless and can kill in minutes. Keep battery CO alarms working inside the home.
  • Never backfeed a house by plugging a generator into a wall outlet or dryer receptacle. It can energize utility lines and kill a lineworker or a neighbor.
  • Connecting to home wiring requires a transfer switch or a listed breaker interlock with a power inlet box, installed by a licensed electrician and permitted where your jurisdiction requires it.
