Can the Champion 4500W Dual Fuel Inverter (200988) run a house through a 30 amp inlet?

Won't run

Not as wired from the factory. A TT-30R is an RV outlet (120 V, 30 A). Home inlets use L14-30 (120/240 V) or L5-30; adapting a TT-30R to a house is not something Willrun recommends without an electrician confirming the inlet and transfer switch.

Questions

Can the Champion 4500W Dual Fuel Inverter (200988) run a house through a 30 amp inlet?

Not as wired from the factory. A TT-30R is an RV outlet (120 V, 30 A). Home inlets use L14-30 (120/240 V) or L5-30; adapting a TT-30R to a house is not something Willrun recommends without an electrician confirming the inlet and transfer switch.

What does a 30 amp inlet supply?

A 30 A, 120/240 V inlet (L14-30) can carry at most 7,200 W, split across two 120 V legs of 3,600 W each. It only powers the circuits your transfer switch or interlock connects.

Do I need an electrician?

Yes. A transfer switch or listed interlock and inlet box must be installed by a licensed electrician. Never backfeed through a wall outlet.

Safety first

  • Run a generator outdoors only, at least 20 feet from the house, with the exhaust pointed away from doors, windows and vents. Never in a garage, basement, shed or crawlspace, even with the door open.
  • Carbon monoxide is odorless and can kill in minutes. Keep battery CO alarms working inside the home.
  • Never backfeed a house by plugging a generator into a wall outlet or dryer receptacle. It can energize utility lines and kill a lineworker or a neighbor.
  • Connecting to home wiring requires a transfer switch or a listed breaker interlock with a power inlet box, installed by a licensed electrician and permitted where your jurisdiction requires it.
