Questions

What number does Willrun compare to the inverter?

Running watts of 1,500 W must fit under continuous output, or under a resistive boost when this load is a simple heater. Published peak is 1,500 W.

How is runtime calculated?

Usable watt-hours (capacity × 0.85) divided by 1,500 W. The duty cycle is 1, derived from the source note, not assumed.
