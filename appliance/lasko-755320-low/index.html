Questions

What number does Willrun compare to the inverter?

Running watts of 900 W must fit under continuous output, or under a resistive boost when this load is a simple heater. Published peak is 900 W.

How is runtime calculated?

Usable watt-hours (capacity × 0.85) divided by 900 W. The duty cycle is 1, derived from the source note, not assumed.

Where did this come from?

Same Lasko listing: energy output 900 W to 1,500 W. Low is the 900 W end of that range. 7.5 A × 120 V = 900 W.
