Questions

What number does Willrun compare to the inverter?

Running watts of 11.8 W must fit under continuous output, or under a resistive boost when this load is a simple heater. Startup watts were not published.

How is runtime calculated?

Usable watt-hours (capacity × 0.85) divided by 11.8 W. The duty cycle is 1, derived from the source note, not assumed.

Where did this come from?

ENERGY STAR certified displays lists LG 27BA450 at 11.80 W on-mode and 0.14 W sleep, 27-inch screen.
