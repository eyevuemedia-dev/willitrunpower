How AC runtime is figured: usable Wh (nameplate × 0.85) ÷ (CPAP watts + about 15 W). The 15 W is the inverter's own draw just to stay on: BLUETTI counts about 15 W of self-consumption in its AC180 runtime formula, and an owner test measured about 17.5 W idle on a DELTA 3 Plus. Larger stations can idle higher, so test your own setup before you rely on it. DC figures skip the inverter and are a best case. Questions

What number does Willrun compare to the inverter?

Running watts of 6.3 W must fit under continuous output, or under a resistive boost when this load is a simple heater. Startup watts were not published.

How is runtime calculated?

On AC: usable watt-hours (capacity × 0.85) divided by (6.3 W + about 15 W of inverter draw). On DC with the maker's converter there is no inverter, so the best case divides by 6.3 W alone. The duty cycle is 1, derived from the source note, not assumed.

Where did this come from?

ResMed's table lists AirMini operation at 6.3 Wh and standby at 1 Wh. Average draw during operation is 6.3 W.
