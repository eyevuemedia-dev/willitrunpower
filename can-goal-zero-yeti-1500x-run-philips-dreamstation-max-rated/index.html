Medical safety notice: Will It Run? gives general power estimates only, not medical advice. Power draw varies by model, settings, pressure, humidifier and altitude. Can the Goal Zero Yeti 1500X run Philips DreamStation, worst case at the 80 W power supply rating?

Runs. Draw is 80 W, within the 2,000 W continuous output. Estimated runtime: about 16.1 hr.

How was the runtime calculated?

1,516 Wh × 0.85 = 1,289 Wh usable, divided by 80 W average draw.

Where did the numbers come from?

Spec list: 1,516 Wh (10.8 V, 140.4 Ah), NMC, 500 cycles to 80% at 1C, 2 AC outlets at 2,000 W and 3,500 W surge. Weight was not in the spec block that was retrieved, so it is omitted. A shelf price was not recorded as a launch price. Philips does not publish a typical DreamStation draw. The DreamStation Heated Humidifier manual lists the 80 W power supply and DC power consumption of 12 VDC at 6.67 A (80 W). Willrun uses that ceiling as a worst case, so real nights will usually last longer. Heated tubing is powered through the humidifier.
