Best power station for an RV

An 8,000 Btu/h BLACK+DECKER window unit draws about 533 W while cooling. Stations at or above 600 W continuous can hold that running load, and the DELTA Pro 3 and F3800 add 120/240 V outlets the smaller packs do not have. None of them publish a match for the compressor's startup watts.

A roof air conditioner is not in this dataset. The closest verified cooling load is a window unit. Runtime below assumes the compressor stays on at the rated input, which is the harsh case, not a mild evening.

Questions

Best power station for an RV?

An 8,000 Btu/h BLACK+DECKER window unit draws about 533 W while cooling. Stations at or above 600 W continuous can hold that running load, and the DELTA Pro 3 and F3800 add 120/240 V outlets the smaller packs do not have. None of them publish a match for the compressor's startup watts.

What does the hours column measure?

Runtime if the 533 W unit stays on. The hours use nameplate watt-hours times 0.85, divided by the average draw named in that column. They are not a lab test of the station.

Why is the list short?

Only stations with a published spec in this set are ranked, and only the first rows are shown. A model that cannot hold the running watts is omitted from the fridge, CPAP, and RV lists. The sump-pump list keeps the stations that will not run it so the cutoff is visible.

Where should I read the caveat?
