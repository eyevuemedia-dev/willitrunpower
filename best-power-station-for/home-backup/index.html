Best power station for home backup

On stored energy alone, the EcoFlow DELTA Pro 3 (4,096 Wh) outlasts the Anker SOLIX F3800 (3,840 Wh), while the F3800 has the higher continuous output at 6,000 W. A 1,500 W Lasko heater needs at least 1,500 W continuous or a documented resistive boost.

Home backup in this set is a fridge, a CPAP, a router, a light, and maybe a heater or a sump pump. It is not a central air conditioner. The 24-hour and 3-day planners add those loads up.

Best power station for home backup?

What does the hours column measure?

Hours at a steady 100 W draw. The hours use nameplate watt-hours times 0.85, divided by the average draw named in that column. They are not a lab test of the station.

Why is the list short?

Only stations with a published spec in this set are ranked, and only the first rows are shown. A model that cannot hold the running watts is omitted from the fridge, CPAP, and RV lists. The sump-pump list keeps the stations that will not run it so the cutoff is visible.
