Best power station for a sump pump

The Zoeller Model 53 at 115 V draws 1,115.5 W while running. Stations under that — including the RIVER 2 series, the AC70, and the Yeti 500X — will not hold it. Stations above it still carry a surge risk because Zoeller does not publish starting amps.

Sump duty cycle depends on inflow. Willrun does not invent a 20% duty cycle. The hours below assume the pump runs continuously, which is the right check for a storm that never lets the float drop.

Questions

Best power station for a sump pump?

The Zoeller Model 53 at 115 V draws 1,115.5 W while running. Stations under that — including the RIVER 2 series, the AC70, and the Yeti 500X — will not hold it. Stations above it still carry a surge risk because Zoeller does not publish starting amps.

What does the hours column measure?

Runtime if the pump never shuts off. The hours use nameplate watt-hours times 0.85, divided by the average draw named in that column. They are not a lab test of the station.

Why is the list short?

Only stations with a published spec in this set are ranked, and only the first rows are shown. A model that cannot hold the running watts is omitted from the fridge, CPAP, and RV lists. The sump-pump list keeps the stations that will not run it so the cutoff is visible.

Where should I read the caveat?
