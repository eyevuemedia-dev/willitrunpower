Questions

Can the WEN DF1100T run ResMed AirSense 11, typical power from the user guide?

Runs. Running draw 56 W fits within 8,300 running watts, and published startup 73 W fits within 11,000 starting watts. This is an open-frame generator; for sensitive electronics or medical devices an inverter generator (under 3% THD) is the safer choice.

What watts were compared?

Generator: 8,300 running W and 11,000 starting W on gasoline. Appliance: 56 W running, 73 W startup.

How long will it run?

Fuel runtime depends on total load. Published runtime: 8.5 hr at 50% load on gasoline, 3 hr on a 20-lb propane tank.

Where did the numbers come from?

WEN product page and manual: gasoline 11000 surge / 8300 running; propane 9500 / 7500; 4 GFCI 5-20R, L5-30R, 14-50R, 12V DC; 6.6 gal; 8.5 hr gas at 50%, 3 hr propane (20 lb). No L14-30R: a 30A 240V inlet needs a 14-50P to L14-30R adapter cord, which Willrun does not assume. Americas user guide, 65 W power supply section: typical power consumption 56.1 W (111.5 VA) and peak power consumption 73.2 W (137.6 VA). This is the published system figure, not the lower no-humidifier row on ResMed's separate therapy-power sheet.

Safety first

  • Run a generator outdoors only, at least 20 feet from the house, with the exhaust pointed away from doors, windows and vents. Never in a garage, basement, shed or crawlspace, even with the door open.
  • Carbon monoxide is odorless and can kill in minutes. Keep battery CO alarms working inside the home.
  • Never backfeed a house by plugging a generator into a wall outlet or dryer receptacle. It can energize utility lines and kill a lineworker or a neighbor.
  • Connecting to home wiring requires a transfer switch or a listed breaker interlock with a power inlet box, installed by a licensed electrician and permitted where your jurisdiction requires it.
