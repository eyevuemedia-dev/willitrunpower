Can the Honda EU2200i run Danby DAR032B1SLM 3.2 cu ft compact refrigerator?

Runs. Running draw 150 W fits within 1,800 running watts. Startup watts were not published; 2,200 starting watts is at least 3× the running draw, the common planning allowance for a compressor or pump start.

What watts were compared?

Generator: 1,800 running W and 2,200 starting W on gasoline. Appliance: 150 W running, startup not published.

How long will it run?

Fuel runtime depends on total load. Published runtime: 3.2 hr at rated load, 8.1 hr at 1/4 load.

Where did the numbers come from?

Honda spec table: 120V 2200W max (18.3A), 1800W rated (15A); 20A 125V duplex; 0.95 gal; 3.2 hr rated / 8.1 hr 1/4 load; 57/48 dB(A); 47.4 lb dry. Danby lists 115 V, 150 W, and 259 kWh/year. Average draw is 29.6 W. Duty cycle 0.197 is the annual average implied by those two figures. Startup watts are not published.

Safety first

  • Run a generator outdoors only, at least 20 feet from the house, with the exhaust pointed away from doors, windows and vents. Never in a garage, basement, shed or crawlspace, even with the door open.
  • Carbon monoxide is odorless and can kill in minutes. Keep battery CO alarms working inside the home.
  • Never backfeed a house by plugging a generator into a wall outlet or dryer receptacle. It can energize utility lines and kill a lineworker or a neighbor.
  • Connecting to home wiring requires a transfer switch or a listed breaker interlock with a power inlet box, installed by a licensed electrician and permitted where your jurisdiction requires it.
